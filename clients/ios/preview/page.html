SHAIP for iPhone

This is the app's own interface, running in your browser: the same screens, words, glass and live 3-D mesh as the iPhone build. The mesh is drawn by the design's render engine, the same file the iPhone renderer is tested against.

A web page can't open a VPN tunnel. When you connect here, the session and its numbers are simulated inside this page, and nothing is sent anywhere. In the iPhone app, every number comes from the tunnel itself.

  1. Use the sample server, or import your own client.toml.
  2. Tap the orb to connect, and again to disconnect.
  3. Open Traffic for the live chart, Protection for what's covered and what isn't.
  4. Show the lock screen to see the Live Activity.
