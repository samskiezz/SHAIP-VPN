# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove
contributions and contributors as necessary.
